# Pioneer League Baseball Analytics & Player Development Shiny App

## Overview

This project is an interactive R Shiny application I developed for the **Ogden Raptors of the Pioneer Baseball League** to centralize baseball analytics, player evaluation, player development, and game-strategy information into one platform.

The application was designed for practical use by coaches and baseball operations staff. Rather than relying on separate spreadsheets, reports, and visualizations, the app provides an interactive environment where users can explore pitcher and hitter performance, evaluate player development, and access information for game preparation.

The application is organized into four primary sections:

1. Pitcher Reports
2. Hitter Reports
3. Development Plans
4. Game Strategy

> **Note:** This repository is a portfolio representation of the application. Player data, team information, credentials, and other proprietary information used in the production version are not publicly available.

## Application Structure

### ⚾ Pitcher Reports

The Pitcher Reports section provides pitch-level analysis for evaluating pitcher performance, arsenal characteristics, command, and results.

#### Pitch Outcomes

Evaluates the results generated by each pitcher's arsenal, allowing performance to be compared across pitch types and situations.

Analysis includes pitch-level outcomes and metrics designed to evaluate how effectively individual pitches generate strikes, swings, misses, and other results.

#### Batted Balls

Examines the quality and characteristics of contact allowed by pitchers.

This section helps evaluate what happens when hitters put individual pitch types into play and provides additional context beyond traditional pitching statistics.

#### Break & Movement

Visualizes pitch movement profiles to evaluate the shape and separation of a pitcher's arsenal.

This section allows coaches and analysts to examine characteristics such as horizontal and vertical movement across pitch types and identify differences within a pitcher's arsenal.

#### Release Point

Visualizes pitcher release characteristics and consistency.

Release-point information can be used to examine mechanical consistency, pitch tunneling, and differences in release characteristics across pitch types.

#### Heat Maps

Provides location-based visualizations of pitch usage and results.

Heat maps allow users to examine where pitchers are locating different pitch types and identify location tendencies and patterns.

---

### 🏏 Hitter Reports

The Hitter Reports section provides an interactive view of offensive performance, swing decisions, contact quality, and hitting mechanics.

#### Batted Balls

Analyzes balls put into play to evaluate offensive contact quality and batted-ball characteristics.

This section provides information that can help identify strengths, weaknesses, and trends in a hitter's offensive profile.

#### Plate Discipline

Evaluates hitter decision-making at the plate.

The section focuses on swing behavior, strike-zone decisions, and other indicators used to understand a hitter's approach.

#### Point of Contact

Examines where hitters make contact relative to pitch location and other pitch characteristics.

This provides additional context for evaluating how hitters interact with different pitches and locations.

#### Blast Motion

Incorporates Blast Motion data to provide additional information about swing characteristics and hitter development.

This section connects sensor-based hitting information with the broader player-development workflow.

#### Heat Maps

Provides location-based visualizations for evaluating hitter performance, swing decisions, and results across different areas of the strike zone.

---

### 📈 Development Plans

The Development Plans section connects analytical findings with individualized player-development objectives.

Rather than limiting the application to descriptive statistics, this section was designed to help translate analytical information into actionable areas of focus for players and coaches.

This allows the platform to function not only as an analytics dashboard, but also as a tool supporting the player-development process.

---

### 🎯 Game Strategy

The Game Strategy section provides information designed to assist coaches with opponent preparation and in-game planning.

#### Pitch Type Summary

Summarizes opponent pitching characteristics and pitch usage to help identify tendencies and prepare hitters for opposing arsenals.

#### Hitter Summary

Provides condensed information on opposing hitters to support pitcher and coaching preparation.

The goal of this section is to translate larger datasets into concise information that can be used during game preparation.
